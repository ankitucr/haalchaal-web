Rubik's Cube Solver — FAQ

Is this 3D Rubik's cube solver free?

Yes, 100% free. The entire Kociemba two-phase solving engine runs inside your browser — no signup, no app install, no server. It even works offline once the page is loaded.

How accurate is the solution?

The solver uses Herbert Kociemba's two-phase algorithm — the same mathematics used by professional cube robots. It finds solutions of roughly 20 moves for any valid scramble.

What do the letters R, U, F, L, D, B mean?

They are face turns: R = Right, L = Left, U = Up, D = Down, F = Front, B = Back — each a 90° clockwise turn looking at that face. An apostrophe (') means counter-clockwise, and 2 means a half turn (180°).

Why does it say my cube state is invalid?

A real cube always has exactly 9 stickers of each color, and pieces can't be twisted or swapped alone. Double-check that your painted colors match your physical cube exactly — one wrong sticker makes the state impossible.
